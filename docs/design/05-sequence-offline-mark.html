Sequence / offline event

Save first; sync when connected

The driver app owns the IndexedDB queue. There is no Background Sync; device-local marks stay durable until server confirmation.

01 / Capture, sync and reconcile

The Chofer selects the Receptor when marking Entregado; the phone applies the cached-consent guard offline.

02 / End a session or revoke a device

Cerrar sesión flushes before ending the session. Revoking a lost device wipes locally without upload; Oficina sees Traslados without a confirmed mark, not queue contents.

03 / Restore and close the day

Restore rotates the server epoch; on mismatch, the phone resends today's confirmed marks and keeps them until end of day.
